The purpose of this simulation is to evaluate the performance of WiFi, Bluetooth, and Cellular communication systems over varying distances by analyzing:

- Received Power

- Signal-to-Interference-plus-Noise Ratio (SINR)

- Channel Capacity

Comparative Insights

Technology	Bandwidth	Range		Capacity
WiFi		High		Short		Very High
Bluetooth	Medium		Very Short	Moderate
Cellular	Low		Very Long	Low

WiFi is ideal for high-speed short-range communication.

Bluetooth is suited for very short-range device connections.

Cellular is designed for wide-area communication with large coverage.

The simulation shows that distance significantly affects wireless communication performance. Technologies with higher bandwidth provide higher capacity but usually operate over shorter ranges, while systems designed for long-range communication sacrifice data rate for coverage.
